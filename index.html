Common questions

Why can’t I just put old electronics in the bin?

Circuit boards and batteries contain lead, cadmium, mercury and flame retardants that leach into soil and water in landfills. Burning them releases toxic fumes. In India, the E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 also make proper disposal the legal route for producers and bulk consumers.

Is my personal data safe?

Wipe your device before handing it over. For storage devices we physically shred drives, and we can issue a destruction certificate on request.

Do you pay for devices?

Working phones and laptops with resale value can earn a credit. Broken items are collected free; we don’t charge households for recycling.

What about companies and schools?

We run scheduled bulk collections with weight reports and recycling certificates that support your sustainability reporting. Use the form and add “bulk” in the notes.

What happens to the plastics and glass?

Plastics are sorted by polymer and shredded for reuse in non-food products. Glass and metals go to smelters and refiners.
